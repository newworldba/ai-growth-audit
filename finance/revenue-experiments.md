## Time Allocation

- 60% on the highest-priority experiment for the week.
- 20% on a second experiment.
- 20% on short-cycle cash opportunities.

Do not add a fourth direction until one active direction either earns money or is paused.

## Evidence Required

Log every experiment with:

1. Date and channel.
2. Qualified person or company.
3. Contact method.
4. Whether they replied.
5. Whether they requested a proposal, paid a deposit, or paid in full.
6. Whether the next action is to continue, iterate, or pause.

## Portfolio Decision

1. Continue an offer only when it creates qualified replies or payments.
2. Iterate the message when people open/read but do not reply.
3. Change the offer when people reply but do not buy.
4. Pause the direction when the trigger above is reached and the alternative has better evidence.
5. Revisit a paused direction only after a new channel, proof asset, or pricing insight appears.
