# Revenue Experiments

## Rule

Do not stay on one direction because it is familiar. Run a small portfolio, review the evidence weekly, and pause any offer that does not create a conversation, proposal, or payment.

## Active Directions

| Direction | Offer | First revenue target | Test budget | Stop / pause trigger | Next action |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| A. Conversion Audit | Founder CRO Kit, Quick Audit, Growth Audit | CNY 199 or CNY 1,080 | CNY 0 | After 20 qualified contacts or 7 days, no reply, demo, or sale. | Publish one useful public micro-audit and promote the scorecard. |
| B. AI Workflow Fix | Turn one repetitive manual workflow into a documented automation blueprint or working browser workflow. | CNY 680 | CNY 0 | After 15 qualified contacts or 7 days, no reply, paid diagnosis, or sale. | Build a focused offer page and free workflow-selection tool. |
| C. Agent Setup Help | Help one solo founder set up a local AI agent workflow and reusable skill library. | CNY 199 | CNY 0 | After 15 qualified posts or 5 days, no reply or sale. | Create one short public setup guide and a paid 45-minute setup session. |

## Time Allocation

- 60% on the highest-priority experiment for the week.
- 20% on a second experiment.
- 20% on short-cycle cash opportunities.

Do not add a fourth direction until one active direction either earns money or is paused.

## Evidence Required

Log every experiment with:

1. Date and channel.
2. Qualified person or company.
3. Contact method.
4. Whether they replied.
5. Whether they requested a proposal, paid a deposit, or paid in full.
6. Whether the next action is to continue, iterate, or pause.

## Portfolio Decision

1. Continue an offer only when it creates qualified replies or payments.
2. Iterate the message when people open/read but do not reply.
3. Change the offer when people reply but do not buy.
4. Pause the direction when the trigger above is reached and the alternative has better evidence.
5. Revisit a paused direction only after a new channel, proof asset, or pricing insight appears.
