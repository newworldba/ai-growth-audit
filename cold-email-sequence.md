# Cold Email Sequence: AI Growth Audit

Send one email per business day. Stop if the recipient replies.

## Email 1 — Day 1

**Subject:** Quick page review for {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I looked at {{page_url}} for a minute before writing. My impression: {{one_specific_observation}}.

I run a focused 48-hour audit for founders. It identifies the top conversion and messaging issues on one key page and gives you the ten fixes to make first.

The price is $149, fixed. No strategy deck—just an actionable report and a short review call.

Want me to send a two-line audit outline before you decide?

Hailong

## Email 2 — Day 2

**Subject:** One question about {{page_url}}

Hi {{first_name}},

When someone lands on {{page_url}}, what action do you want them to take?

I ask because a page can look good but still lose people if the first screen does not make the next step obvious.

If useful, I can send a five-minute review showing where visitors may hesitate.

Hailong

## Email 3 — Day 3

**Subject:** Three things I would check first

Hi {{first_name}},

For pages like {{page_url}}, I usually check:

1. Whether the first screen names the right customer.
2. Whether the CTA explains what happens next.
3. Whether proof appears before the visitor is asked to commit.

The full audit turns those into a prioritized 48-hour fix list.

Want the link to book?

Hailong

## Email 4 — Day 5

**Subject:** Closing the loop

I know timing matters, so I’ll stop here for now.
