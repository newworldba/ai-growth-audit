# Outreach Plan

## Ideal Clients

- SaaS founders with a public pricing page and unclear positioning.
- DTC stores selling a single high-consideration product.
- Course creators with a sales page but weak CTA hierarchy.
- Service businesses with a homepage that buries the offer.

## Where to Find Them

1. Product Hunt recent launches.
2. Indie Hackers founder threads.
3. LinkedIn posts about launching a product or improving conversion.
4. GitHub project sites with a public landing page.
5. Online stores using public storefront platforms.

Only contact businesses that publish a business contact address or explicitly welcome inquiries. Follow CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and local anti-spam rules.

## Daily Routine — 60 Minutes

1. Find 20 companies.
2. Visit each page for 60 seconds.
3. Write one specific observation.
4. Send 10 personalized cold emails.
5. Connect with 5 relevant founders on LinkedIn if their profile invites professional contact.
6. Track company, contact, date, reply, and next step.

## Message Rules

- Sell one page audit, not “AI services.”
- Mention one specific detail from their site.
- Avoid fake urgency.
- Avoid revenue guarantees.
- Keep the first email under 120 words.
- Follow up twice, then stop.

## Objection Handling

**“We already have a designer.”**
> Great. I complement design work by prioritizing the changes most likely to affect conversion and messaging.

**“We don’t have enough traffic yet.”**
> That makes the audit even more useful before you spend money on traffic.

**“Can you just do the changes?”**
> Yes. After the audit, we can scope an implementation sprint.
