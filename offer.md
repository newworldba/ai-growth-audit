# AI Growth Audit Offer

## Positioning

**Offer:** A 48-hour AI-assisted growth audit for websites, landing pages, and online stores.

**Primary customers:**
- Independent SaaS founders
- DTC / e-commerce brands
- Course creators and service businesses
- Local companies selling outside China

**Core promise:** Identify the highest-impact conversion, SEO, and messaging issues, then give a prioritized action list a founder can execute without hiring a full marketing team.

## Why This Is Legal and Low-Risk

- You sell analysis, recommendations, and implementation help—not unauthorized access, account manipulation, or platform-policy bypasses.
- You only audit pages the client owns or authorizes you to review.
- You do not promise guaranteed revenue.
- You do not fabricate case studies.
- You comply with the client's privacy policy and applicable data-protection rules.

## Packages

### 1. Quick Audit — USD 149

**Delivery time:** 48 hours

Includes:
- Review of one landing page or product page.
- Conversion-blocking issue list.
- Top 10 prioritized fixes.
- Suggested headline, CTA, and proof-section improvements.
- 30-minute follow-up call or async video walkthrough.

### 2. Growth Audit — USD 399

**Delivery time:** 5 business days

Includes:
- Up to 5 key pages.
- Conversion, messaging, SEO basics, and mobile usability review.
- Competitor positioning snapshot for up to 3 public competitors.
- 30-day prioritized action plan.
- Copy rewrite suggestions for key sections.
- 60-minute consultation.

### 3. Implementation Sprint — USD 999

**Delivery time:** 10 business days

Includes:
- Everything in Growth Audit.
- Direct implementation of up to 10 approved changes.
- Before/after measurement checklist.
- Analytics event checklist.
- Two revision rounds.
- 2 consultation calls.

## Delivery Workflow

1. Client submits the site URL, target customer, main conversion goal, and analytics access if available.
2. You confirm scope and payment.
3. You audit the authorized pages only.
4. You deliver a concise report with issue, impact, evidence, and recommended fix.
5. You review the report with the client.
6. For implementation packages, you get written approval before changing content.

## Sales Assets to Publish

- Landing page.
- Lead magnet checklist.
- Cold email sequence.
- Outreach targets and follow-up plan.
- Launch checklist.

## First Legal Money Action

Offer five discovery calls to founders with live websites. Do not sell a vague "AI service." Sell a specific audit with a clear price, scope, and deliverable.
