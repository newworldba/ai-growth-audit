26-Point Landing Page Audit Checklist

Use this checklist before you spend money on traffic. Work through one page at a time, mark each item as yes or no, and fix the first three gaps—not every item at once.

1. Clarity

  1. The first five words say who the page is for.
  2. The first screen states the customer outcome, not just the product category.
  3. The primary action is visible without scrolling.
  4. Only one primary action competes for attention.
  5. A visitor can explain what you offer after five seconds.

2. Offer and CTA

  1. The primary CTA uses a result-focused verb.
  2. The secondary CTA does not compete with the primary action.
  3. The form asks only for necessary fields.
  4. The submit button describes the result.
  5. The visitor knows what happens after clicking.

3. Trust and objections

  1. Relevant proof appears near the decision point.
  2. Proof is specific, not generic praise.
  3. Setup effort is explained.
  4. Privacy or data boundaries are explained.
  5. The main objection is answered near the CTA.

4. Pricing and risk

  1. Price or plan boundaries are easy to find.
  2. What is included is clear.
  3. What is excluded is clear.
  4. Refund or cancellation policy is visible.
  5. The page explains who should not buy.

5. Mobile and speed

  1. The primary mobile action is visible on first load.
  2. Tap targets are at least 44px.
  3. Forms are usable on a phone.
  4. The page loads without blocking visual issues.

6. Search and measurement

  1. The title and meta description match buyer intent.
  2. You know this week’s success event.
