# Website Conversion Checklist: 25 Fixes Founders Can Apply Today

Use this checklist on one page at a time. Mark each item as **Good**, **Needs work**, or **Missing**.

## Positioning

1. The first screen says who the product is for.
2. The first screen explains the main outcome.
3. The headline avoids vague words like “innovative,” “powerful,” and “next-generation.”
4. The primary call to action appears above the fold.
5. The page has one obvious next step.

## Offer Clarity

6. The visitor can tell what they receive after purchase or signup.
7. Pricing or the pricing process is easy to find.
8. The page explains who should not buy.
9. The offer removes one specific objection.
10. The CTA text describes the action, not just “Submit.”

## Trust

11. The business explains who is behind the product.
12. The page uses specific proof, not generic claims.
13. Testimonials mention the customer’s situation, not only praise.
14. Contact information is easy to find.
15. Refund, cancellation, or support terms are visible.

## Page Structure

16. Each section answers one visitor question.
17. The page is readable without zooming on a phone.
18. The primary CTA is repeated at natural decision points.
19. The page avoids long blocks of text.
20. Forms request only necessary information.

## SEO Basics

21. The title tag names the page’s main topic and audience.
22. The meta description explains the benefit and invites a click.
23. The main heading matches the page intent.
24. Internal links help visitors reach pricing, docs, or contact pages.
25. Images have useful alt text and do not delay the first screen.

## How to Use This as a Lead Magnet

- Put the checklist behind a simple email form.
- Send it immediately after signup.
- Follow up in 48 hours with one question: “Which item looked weakest on your page?”
