# Payment confirmation template

Use this after the client pays. Store one copy in your client tracker and send one copy to the client.

```
Order: Audit request #{issue_number}
Client: {client_name}
Website: {website_url}
Package: {package_name}
Amount paid: {amount} {currency}
Payment method: Alipay / WeChat Pay
Payment reference: {transaction_last_4_digits_or_order_number}
Paid at: {date_and_time}
Delivery due: {delivery_date}
Scope confirmed: {pages_or_deliverables}
```

Do not store payment passwords, full bank account numbers, identity numbers, or other sensitive client data.
