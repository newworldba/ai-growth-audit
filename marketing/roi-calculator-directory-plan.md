## Positioning variants

### Launch directories

**Tagline:** Estimate conversion revenue before buying traffic.

**Description:**  
Conversion ROI Calculator is the easiest way to estimate the revenue impact of a conversion-rate improvement for founders and small teams. Enter monthly visitors, current conversion rate, average revenue per conversion, and expected uplift; the browser-only tool shows current revenue, projected revenue, and incremental monthly or annual impact. No upload, no account, and no revenue promise.

**Tags:** conversion optimization, CRO calculator, revenue planning, marketing calculator, browser tool

### SaaS directories

**Tagline:** A lightweight alternative to spreadsheet ROI estimates.

**Description:**  
Conversion ROI Calculator helps SaaS founders and marketers turn conversion assumptions into a revenue forecast. Instead of rebuilding a spreadsheet, users enter four inputs and get monthly, annual, and incremental revenue estimates. The result can be copied directly into an audit request, making it useful for planning traffic spend or evaluating a CRO experiment.

**Tags:** CRO alternative, SaaS calculator, marketing analytics, revenue forecast, conversion audit

### Developer / technical directories

**Tagline:** Browser-only conversion math with zero tracking.

**Description:**  
Conversion ROI Calculator is a static, browser-only tool for estimating the revenue impact of conversion-rate changes. It has no backend, no upload, and no account requirement. The result is exposed as plain text for copying and can be passed into a prefilled audit request as URL parameters, making it easy to share in technical teams.

**Tags:** browser tool, static site, CRO, marketing calculator, privacy-friendly
