Founder CRO Kit

A practical kit for founders and small teams who need to find conversion blockers, rewrite key copy, and build a 30-day action plan. Work through it in order; do not try to fix everything at once.

GrowthAudit home

1. Start with the 10-question scorecard

Answer yes or no for one landing page. Any “no” is a candidate fix, but only work on the first three.

Clarity
  1. First five words show who it’s for.
  2. First screen states the outcome.
  3. Next action is obvious in five seconds.
Action
  1. Primary CTA describes the result.
  2. Only one primary action competes for attention.
  3. Price or plan boundaries are clear.
Trust
  1. Relevant proof appears near the decision point.
  2. Objections about setup, privacy, or effort are answered.
  3. Contact or support path is easy to find.
Delivery
  1. Mobile action is obvious and tap-friendly.
  2. Title and description match buyer intent.
  3. You know this week’s success event.

Headline patterns

  • Audience + outcome: For boutique fitness studios, fill empty classes without discounting.
  • Problem + mechanism: Stop chasing receipts. Connect Gmail, AWS, and Stripe once.
  • Before + after: Turn a product page into a 60-second demo reel.
  • Specific time: Get a prioritized Top 10 fix list in 48 hours.
  • Alternative: The self-hosted alternative to scattered analytics tools.

4. Answer the three buying objections

Most pages lose action because they answer features, not doubts. Pick the three objections that actually appear in sales calls.

ObjectionWhere it appearsExact reassurance
“This looks hard to set up.”Near primary CTA
“Can I trust this with my data?”Near form or payment
“Will this work for my situation?”Near pricing or showcase

5. Build the 30-day action plan

Week 1 — clarity

  • Fix headline and subheadline.
  • Rewrite primary CTA.
  • Remove competing links.
  • Check mobile first screen.

Week 2 — trust

  • Add one relevant proof element near CTA.
  • Answer top three objections.
  • Clarify pricing boundaries.
  • Check contact path.

Week 3 — activation

  • Reduce form fields.
  • Fix mobile tap targets.
  • Clarify onboarding steps.
  • Add a sample output or demo.

Week 4 — measurement

  • Define one conversion event.
  • Review visitor-to-action rate.
  • Keep what improved.
  • Revert what did not.
